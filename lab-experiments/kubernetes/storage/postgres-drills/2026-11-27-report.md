## An alert at rest after the drills

At 22:38 `PrometheusMissingRuleEvaluations` fired and was sent to the alert channel. It was not a drill. It resolved at 22:42 and went pending again at 22:53.

- **Found by:** the Discord counter moved from 8 to 10 after the last drill had ended. `ALERTS` over the previous 45 minutes named the rule.
- **Symptom:** `kube-apiserver-availability.rules` took up to 164 s against a 180 s interval, and `kube-apiserver-burnrate.rules` up to 67 s against a 30 s interval. An hour earlier they took 9 s and 2 to 5 s. Longhorn reported reads of about 200 MiB/s from the Prometheus volume.
- **Cause:** the Prometheus container was at its 900Mi limit with 605 MiB of process memory and 286 MiB of page cache, so the rules that look back 3 and 30 days re-read blocks from disk on every evaluation. The live series had grown from 81,282 to 104,740 in three hours. 17,455 of the new series were API server histograms, which grow with every CRD and every new kind of request; the operator alone added eleven CRDs.
- **Fix:** [#115](https://github.com/PragalvaXFREZ/lab/pull/115) raised the limit to 1400Mi and dropped six API server histogram families that no rule or dashboard reads (21,946 series).
- **After:** Prometheus restarted at 22:57:53. At 23:07 the two groups took 0.16 s and 1.65 s, no evaluation was missed, nothing was pending, and the container held 814 MiB of page cache. The API server job went from about 50,000 live series to 28,490.

The lesson for the next operator or CRD-heavy chart: check `count by (job) ({__name__=~".+"})` and `container_memory_cache` for Prometheus before and after.
